Read with care

Caveats

  • 2024-08 and 2026-02 counts are inflated: Wayback rows carry crawl timestamps, not publish dates, so archived batches pile onto those months.
  • 2026-08 is a partial month (163 articles): its swings are small-n noise; endpoints are drawn hollow.
  • Input Costs is the one finBERT trap: "cement prices soar" reads positive to a finance model. Read direction, not level, in that bucket.
  • Equity-market coverage was deliberately dropped (1,402 articles): stock moves measure investor mood, not whether anything gets built. Housing finance was kept: mortgage credit is real demand.
